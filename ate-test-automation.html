Automated Test Equipment

End-to-end ATE solutions built on NI technology and best-in-class hardware

Bosevia designs, develops, and deploys Automated Test Equipment (ATE) systems for defence, aerospace, and industrial applications — integrating NI platforms with third-party OEM hardware and LabVIEW-based software to deliver precise, reliable, and scalable test solutions.

With deep domain knowledge and hands-on experience across mission-critical programmes, we work as a trusted technology partner — from initial requirement analysis through to system integration, commissioning, and long-term support.

What We Do

ATE system development from concept to commissioning

We partner with clients across the full ATE lifecycle, translating test requirements into deployable, validated systems.

Our core service areas:

Test system design

Architecture design for functional test, production test, and acceptance test systems aligned with client specifications, standards, and regulatory requirements.

LabVIEW software development

Custom LabVIEW applications for instrument control, data acquisition, signal analysis, test sequencing, and reporting — using NI's full software stack.

Hardware integration

Seamless integration of NI PXI/PXIe chassis, NI-DAQ, NI-VISA instruments, and third-party OEM hardware including signal generators, power supplies, and switching matrices.

Test automation & sequencing

Automated test execution using NI TestStand with structured sequence management, result logging, report generation, and database integration.

Validation & verification support

Support for HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) testing, subsystem V&V, and acceptance testing for defence and aerospace programmes.

System upgrades & migration

Modernisation of legacy ATE systems — hardware refresh, LabVIEW version migration, driver updates, and re-platforming to current NI ecosystems.

Domains Served

ATE expertise across critical industries

Our ATE solutions span defence electronics, aerospace avionics, RF & communications, power electronics, and industrial automation — each requiring precise measurement capability and rigorous test engineering.

Defence

Radar sub-system testing, EW equipment validation, avionics LRU test, and DO-178 / MIL-STD aligned test environments.

Aerospace

Functional test benches for flight electronics, engine controls, and structural health monitoring data acquisition systems.

RF & Communications

RF parametric testing, signal characterisation, transmitter/receiver validation using vector signal analysers and software-defined radio platforms.

Industrial & Power electronics

End-of-line production test, power electronics characterisation, motor drive testing, and PLC/SCADA integration for industrial clients.

How we deliver

From requirement to long-term support

  1. Requirement analysis

    We turn your test specification, standards and UUT interfaces into a clear test requirement baseline.

  2. Architecture and design

    Instruments, switching, UUT interface and software architecture are selected and reviewed with you.

  3. Build and integration

    LabVIEW and TestStand software is developed and integrated with the chosen NI and OEM hardware.

  4. Validation and commissioning

    The system is verified against requirements and commissioned at your site.

  5. Long-term support

    Upgrades, driver updates, calibration support and migration as your programme evolves.
