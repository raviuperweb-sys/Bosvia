About Bosevia Technologies

Bosevia at a Glance

Bosevia Technologies Pvt Ltd is a Bangalore-based software startup launched in September 2025, built to help organizations optimize efficiency, enhance reliability, and accelerate growth through innovative technology solutions.

Our expertise spans Aerospace & Avionics, Automotive, Embedded Systems, Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals, BFSI, Telecommunications, and web-based applications — partnering with enterprises across multiple industries to solve real business challenges.

We are a dynamic team dedicated to delivering cutting-edge IT consulting, outsourcing, and technology services to clients across a wide range of industries.

At Bosevia, we believe in creating value through innovation, quality, and reliability. Our skilled professionals bring experience in software development, enterprise solutions, cloud computing, data analytics, and IT infrastructure management.

Whether developing next-generation applications, managing complex IT projects, or providing skilled technical resources, Bosevia Technologies stands committed to excellence and client satisfaction.

Company facts

  • Incorporated on 25th September 2025
  • Headquartered in Bangalore, India
  • Clients across defence, aerospace, industrial and enterprise sectors
  • Services: IT consulting, outsourcing, software and ATE engineering
