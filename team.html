Our Leadership

The Minds Behind Bosevia

Our management team leads strategic innovation and long-term growth aligned with Bosevia's core vision and mission.

Leadership Excellence

Jagadeesh Chandra Bose

Chairman & Managing Director

Jagadeesh Chandra Bose is the Founder, Chairman & Managing Director of Bosevia Technologies Pvt. Ltd., bringing over two decades of comprehensive experience in the IT industry. He holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Bangalore University, with a strong foundation in Human Resources and strategic management.

Throughout his career, Mr. Bose has partnered with leading global and Indian organizations, delivering high-impact talent acquisition and workforce solutions. His expertise encompasses end-to-end recruitment, enterprise staffing strategy, resource delivery management, and workforce planning across permanent and contract hiring models.

He leads Bosevia Technologies with a clear strategic vision focused on operational excellence, resilient team development, and the delivery of future-ready talent solutions aligned with evolving industry demands.

Prior to establishing Bosevia Technologies, Mr. Bose held senior leadership positions including Senior HR Manager & Administration and Resource Delivery Manager, where he led high-performing teams, managed large-scale hiring programs, and strengthened client partnerships through structured governance frameworks.

Ambli Suresh

Chief Operating Officer (COO) & Mentor

Ambli Suresh holds an M.Tech in Structures and is a seasoned executive with over four decades of extensive experience in engineering, project leadership, and professional consultancy. He also holds prestigious certifications including Chartered Engineer, FIE, Registered Valuer, and FACI.

He has successfully planned, designed, and executed major industrial and infrastructure projects, including international training facilities, export-oriented manufacturing units, and a Special Economic Zone.

An IIT Bombay alumnus, he brings a strong command of strategic direction, operational governance, and organizational stewardship. At Bosevia Technologies, he contributes his executive perspective to reinforce the company's strategic intent, governance framework, and long-term operational resilience.

Dr. S. Prabhu

Mentor

Dr. S. Prabhu holds a BE in Civil Engineering, MTech in Industrial Structures, and a PhD in Civil Engineering from the University of Mysore and Jain University, complemented by advanced training at INSEAD Singapore, Ramboll Denmark, and IRICEN Pune.

He has three decades of distinguished experience in infrastructure engineering and project management. His career highlights include serving as Chief Track Engineer for South Western Railway and Chief Engineer in Bengaluru.

At Bosevia, Dr. Prabhu provides mentorship on integrating advanced technologies into infrastructure projects, including BIM modelling, digital twins, and IT-enabled infrastructure simulations.

Sunil Kumar V

Director & Chief Technology Officer

Sunil Kumar V is a distinguished technology leader with over 23 years of experience in Automated Test Equipment (ATE), LabVIEW programming, and advanced test & measurement systems. He is the Founder and CEO of VI Solutions, where he has successfully built and led a high-performance engineering team, delivering cutting-edge solutions across diverse industries.

Sunil began his career at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) and has worked with leading organizations. He brings deep domain expertise from executing numerous mission-critical projects for premier defense organizations such as HAL, DRDO, DLRL, ELSEC, SFC, and RCI, demonstrating strong capabilities in handling complex, high-reliability systems.

Internationally, he has contributed to advanced aerospace programs with Meggitt Aircraft Braking Systems (United Kingdom), working on projects aligned with stringent global standards.

As Director, he provides strategic leadership, drives innovation, and steers the organization toward scalable, future-ready solutions aligned with Industry 4.0 and emerging technologies.

Dinabandhu Behera

Chief Technology Officer (CTO) & Mentor

Dinabandhu Behera serves as Chief Technology Officer steering the company's strategic vision in growing the Aerospace and Defence portfolio. He has over 30 years of elite experience in the aerospace and defence sectors.

A specialist in DO-178C, DO-254, and complex hardware-software integration, Dinabandhu has built an illustrious career spanning India's premier defence labs and global technology giants, including HAL, DRDO, Thales, IBM, and BAeHAL.

By combining high-level research insights with commercial innovation, he ensures that every project meets the most rigorous safety-critical standards.

S.R. Subramanya

Mentor
